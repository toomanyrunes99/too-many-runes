# Too Many Runes - Changelog

## 3.3.43

- **Hover boxes like the game's:** hovering an item shows a box with the game's own border, size, text and colors. Stats are compared with what you're wearing (green better, red worse).
- **More in the hover box:** potions, food, pickaxes, summoning pouches and scrolls, relics, currencies, Archaeology mysteries and farm animals.
- **The item list follows you:** opening an item from Uses, Recipe, the Back arrow, Recently viewed or a right-click menu jumps the list to it (its stack unfolds if needed).
- **"Hidden by" line:** an item kept out of the list by a Settings switch says which one.
- **New Settings page:** four tabs, a highlight on the row you're on, green On / dim Off, and a color picker with a live preview for your own hover color.
- **Loading speeds:** a new Turbo speed puts each page up in one go. Smooth stays the default.
- **Stays on your page** while the plugin is still loading.
- **Fits your screen:** the window shrinks and moves back on screen when the game window gets smaller or moves to a smaller monitor.
- **Recently viewed** button has its own hover box.
- **Player-owned farm:** the mushes say how they're made and that they go in the trough; the trough's own copies of foods are hidden.
- **Tidier tabs:** Crucible and Runespan siphoning items moved to Minigames, plus a few single items moved to the right skill.
- **More junk hidden:** bank boosters, returner packs, founder packs and other shop placeholders.
- **Fixes:** button and tab colors no longer turn white when hovered, long names and lines wrap instead of being cut off, and many smaller fixes.

## 3.2.62

- **New tabs:** every item is sorted into groups (Gear, Food, Gathering, Artisan, Support, Quests, Misc) with smaller groups inside.
- **Favorites tab:** your starred items, right next to All.
- **Minigames group:** with a setting to hide minigame items.
- **Wiki recipes:** thousands of items the game has no recipe for now show one from the RuneScape Wiki.
- **Older quest items:** shown with their quest, and searchable with `@quest`.
- **Smarter stacks:** urns, salvage, armour sets and item forms stack together.
- **Smoother loading:** no more freeze while the plugin starts up.
- **Lots of fixes:** hundreds of items moved to the right group, plus menu and loading glitches fixed.

## 3.2.03

- **Item stacks:** the different forms of an item now share one picture with a number on it. Click it to unfold the rest. This covers doses and charges, augmented, dyed and broken versions, upgrades (+ 1 to + 5, Mk. 1 to Mk. 5) and stages, such as the Eternal magic orbs.
- **Made in the backpack:** items you can make without a station show "Made in: Backpack". Search them with `@backpack`.
- **Compost:** see what goes in a compost bin and which compost it makes, with `@compostable`, `@supercompostable` and `@ultracompostable`.
- **Quest items:** search with `@quest`, or `@quest` plus a quest's name. The info box shows which quest an item is from.
- **New search words:**
  - item kinds: `@ore`, `@bar`, `@log`, `@fish`, `@herb`, `@seed`, `@gem`, `@rune`;
  - filters: `@f2p`, `@members`, `@ge`, `@untradeable`;
  - right-click options: `@teleport`, `@bury`, `@read` and more.
- **More station pictures:** the small crafting stations and Aggie now have pictures (`@aggie`).
- **Less clutter:** more junk is hidden automatically, including Dungeoneering copies, Twitch drops, "dummy" items and pickpocket guides.
- **Smoother:** typing in the search box no longer lags, and the mouse wheel down now goes to the next page.
- **Lighter:** the plugin uses about 50-60 MB of memory instead of about 250 MB. It loads faster, and reloading no longer slows the game down.
- **Fixes:**
  - long debug names wrap instead of being cut off;
  - "Hide junk" updates the open item straight away;
  - "Reset to default" now says so in the console;
  - the right-click entries can never become the left-click action.
